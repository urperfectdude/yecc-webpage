FRESHER TRAINING COHORT

A structured eight-week path from preparation to presentation.

Each week combines self-paced portal learning, assessed practice, and live trainer support—building toward mock CRPs and a final presentation.

View Upcoming Cohorts
01
Weekly preparation

Watch the assigned learning videos

Trainees follow the weekly schedule and complete the required portal videos before each planned session. The content balances Oracle functional knowledge with consulting-skill development.

02
Knowledge checks

Complete quizzes and practice assignments

After each video, trainees complete an MCQ quiz and one or more descriptive practice assignments. The YECC team reviews submitted answers and monitors progress.

03
Instructor guidance

Join live online doubt-solving sessions

Once the weekly videos, quizzes, and assignments are complete, trainees attend expert-led online sessions to clarify concepts, discuss the exercises, and resolve doubts.

04
Cohort readiness

Prepare for mock CRPs and presentations

During week seven of the eight-week cohort, YECC helps trainees prepare for mock CRPs. In the final week, presentation topics are assigned across the batch for structured practice.
