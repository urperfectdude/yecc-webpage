Curriculum

Build working knowledge across 7 modules.

The curriculum starts with ERP foundations, then moves into the functional modules used across the Oracle Cloud Financials pathway.

01Basics of ERP
02Common Basic Concepts
03General Accounting (GL)
04Accounts Payable (AP)
05Accounts Receivable (AR)
06Cash Management (CM)
07Fixed Assets (FA)

What is included

Detailed self-paced videos cover all topics in the listed modules, supported by practical checks and trainer guidance.

Self-paced learning

Detailed portal videos support the topics covered in the program.

Quizzes and assignments

MCQ tests and one or more hands-on practice assignments reinforce each topic.

Oracle demo access

Use an Oracle Cloud ERP demo system for hands-on practice during the training period.

Interview preparation

Get support preparing for Oracle Cloud Functional Consultant interviews.

Delivery approach

Learn on a weekly rhythm that keeps practice moving.

Each cycle moves from preparation to hands-on practice and trainer-led clarification.

01
Prepare

Complete the scheduled self-study

Watch the required portal videos before each planned session date.

02
Practise

Test your understanding

Complete an MCQ quiz and one or more portal assignments that involve hands-on system practice.

03
Clarify

Join expert-led live sessions

Bring your completed videos, quizzes, and assignments into live online sessions where trainers resolve doubts.
