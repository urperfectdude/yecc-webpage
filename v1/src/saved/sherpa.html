THE AUTHORING AND DELIVERY LAYER

Create once. Govern centrally. Deliver learning by role.

SHERPA is YECC's IP platform for creating user manuals and process videos, delivering role-based learning, and keeping enablement available beyond go-live.

Discuss a SHERPA pilot
OUR SHERPA DELIVERY APPROACH

Built around the client’s Oracle solution.

YECC works alongside the implementation partner to deliver user-training workshops and materials specific to the organisation’s processes, configuration, and users.

01

We understand the solution

We analyse the organisation’s user manuals and Oracle Cloud test environment, then engage business users to understand processes, roles, and operational pain points.

02

We create and iterate the content

Using the client’s data, business processes, and Oracle Cloud test environment, YECC develops customised training videos, workshop materials, and supporting learning content.

03

We deliver through a dedicated portal

Personalised Oracle Cloud ERP training video bundles are deployed through a dedicated SHERPA learning portal configured for the organisation and its learners.
