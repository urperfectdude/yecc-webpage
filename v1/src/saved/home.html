ORACLE SYSTEM INTEGRATOR PARTNERSHIPS

Extend your delivery team with a dedicated user enablement workstream.

YECC works alongside your SI team to turn configured processes into practical documentation, training, and sustained access through SHERPA.

01

SHERPA for Implementation

Bring YECC into your implementation workstream to manage documentation and user enablement from process design through go-live.

  • Capture configured processes as project decisions are approved
  • Prepare key users before the wider end-user rollout
  • Deliver role-based manuals, process videos, and training
  • Launch SHERPA as the user knowledge hub for go-live
02

SHERPA for AMS

Extend your AMS services with ongoing user enablement, refresher training, and updated documentation as processes and Oracle Cloud ERP releases evolve.

  • Refresh business users as processes and releases change
  • Update manuals and process videos under governed standards
  • Deliver targeted retraining for affected roles
  • Keep current guidance available through SHERPA

SHERPA connects content creation with user adoption.

SHERPA is more than a static LMS. It is YECC’s authoring and delivery platform for turning implementation knowledge into governed user manuals, process videos, and role-based learning experiences.
